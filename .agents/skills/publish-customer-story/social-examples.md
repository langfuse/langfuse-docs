# Social post examples

These are the house examples. Match their shape. Do not copy their facts into a different company's post.

LinkedIn is a hook, a short paragraph on what the company builds, a "today they use Langfuse" bridge, three or four `✔️` result lines, then a pointer to the story. The URL can sit on its own line or be "link in comments".

X is shorter: a hook of one or two lines, one sentence on the Langfuse result, then `https://langfuse.com/users/<slug>`. Tag the company only when you know the real handle.

## LinkedIn

```
"Langfuse allowed us to go from reliable changes every few months to iterating multiple times a week on the performance and cost of our platform."

Slite builds an AI knowledge base that maintains itself: it is powered by an agent that proofreads, moves, and archives documents without being asked. Because its agents act on company internals, observability and data residency were non-negotiable from the start.

Today, Slite self-hosts Langfuse and evaluates every change to its agents, with impressive results:
✔️ A CEO-run spreadsheet replaced by a ~100-item Langfuse dataset
✔️ LLM-as-a-judge grading expected behavior instead of exact answers
✔️ Experiments up from once a quarter to once a week, roughly 12x
✔️ A retrospective agent reading production traces via the API at ~10k questions a day

Check out the full story on our blog, link in comments 👇
```

When the [Langfuse Assistant launched in June](/changelog/2026-06-19-langfuse-assistant-public-beta), it was a way to ask questions about your data. Since then it has grown & evolved: Now it can take actions on your behalf, it knows what you are currently looking & working at, and it now lives in the top navigation.

A few things you can ask it to do:

- "Set up an LLM-as-a-judge evaluator for this prompt."
- "Create a dashboard for cost broken down by model."
- "Cluster the failures in these traces and derive an evaluator from them."

## Takes action, with your approval [#takes-action]

The Assistant now has access to the full [Langfuse MCP](/docs/api-and-data-platform/features/mcp-server) tool set. It can work with prompts, datasets, score configs, evaluators, annotation queues, and — as of last week — [dashboards and widgets](/changelog/2026-07-17-dashboard-and-widget-operations-in-api).

Reads run automatically. Every write action pauses for your explicit approval before it happens. It also draws on structured Langfuse skills, such as error analysis, to carry out multi-step workflows rather than one-off calls.

## Knows what you're looking at [#context-aware]

The Assistant is aware of your current context in the UI, including the trace, observation, session, prompt, or dashboard you have open. So "why is this trace slow?" or "add this to a dataset" just works, without copy-pasting IDs into the chat. Also the suggested 1uick actions are context-aware too.

## One keystroke away [#top-nav]

Assistant is now part of the top navigation, and you can toggle the Assistant open or closed with `Cmd+I` (macOS) or `Ctrl+I` (elsewhere) and you can always minimize it while it works.

## Coming soon: code execution [#sandbox]

We're working on a sandboxed environment where the Assistant can run code to work through large volumes of traces and data more efficiently. More on that soon.

## Get started

The Assistant is available on Langfuse Cloud and is still in beta. We'd love your feedback in our [feedback discussion](https://github.com/orgs/langfuse/discussions/14196).

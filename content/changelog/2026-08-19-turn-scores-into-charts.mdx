You already filter the Scores table down to the rows you care about. Now you can turn that view into a chart in one click.

Use **Table | Chart** to visualize numeric, boolean, or categorical scores over time. Choose a metric, aggregation, breakdown, and chart type in the **Visualize** panel.

The **Pulse** strip highlights unusual scores, so you can jump straight to the underlying rows. When a view is worth keeping, **Add to dashboard** saves it as a dashboard widget.

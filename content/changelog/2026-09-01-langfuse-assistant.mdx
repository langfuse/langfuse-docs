The [Langfuse Assistant](/docs/langfuse-assistant) can now take on whole tasks in Langfuse. Describe what you want and it creates the resource, runs the code, and reports back:

- "Create a dataset from the failed generations of last week."
- "Build a dashboard of error rate and p95 latency for the last 7 days."
- "Review this prompt against current best practices and propose a tighter version."

It can also work through hundreds or thousands of observations at once. The Assistant fetches them and runs code over the results in a sandbox, rather than loading every payload into the model, so you can ask it to find a failure pattern, extract a field from every response, or cluster similar outputs.

Every run happens in the background, independently of your browser. Keep exploring Langfuse or start a second conversation while the first one is still working. You get a notification when a run finishes, fails, or needs your input.

Anything that changes data or configuration still waits for your approval. For repeating work in one conversation, choose **Always approve** on a tool you are comfortable leaving unattended, and the Assistant uses that tool for the rest of that conversation without asking again.

The Assistant is available on Langfuse Cloud when AI features are enabled for your organization. Organization admins can enable them in the organization settings.

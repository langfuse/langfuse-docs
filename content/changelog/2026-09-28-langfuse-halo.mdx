In June, we launched Langfuse Assistant to query your Langfuse project data. In July, the assistant could make changes to your langfuse project and since August, the assistant can run in the background. Finally, since the beginning of September, the assistant got a sandbox to scale data analysis to thousands of traces.

The assistant is not a simple assistant anymore, but can do work and watch over your traces. It evolved to become Langfuse Halo.

Halo is a native way to interact with Langfuse:
- "Find what the slowest 100 traces from yesterday have in common."
- "Create a dataset from the failed generations of last week."
- "Build a dashboard of error rate and p95 latency for the last 7 days."
- and a _lot_ more.

Halo queries trace data, metrics, Langfuse documentation, and picks up context from the page you are viewing.
It can create/update datasets, dashboards, widgets, prompts, score configs and any other data.

You can trigger long running analysis in Halo and it reliably runs in a sandbox in the background. You're notified by your browser when it requires approval or concludes the analysis.

Actions that change data or configuration pause for your approval. Choose **Always approve** for a tool to reuse that approval within the current conversation. Halo stays within your personal permissions.

Langfuse Halo is powered by our MCP server, so you can get all capabilities within your local coding agent, too: [Langfuse MCP server](/docs/mcp).

Activate Langfuse Halo for your organization in the [AI feature](/security/ai-features) settings.

Langfuse Halo is fully supported in self-hosted setups since `v4.28.0`, see: **[Self-hosted Langfuse Halo](/docs/self-hosting/configuration/langfuse-halo)**

You can now see how your organization's usage develops over time and which projects drive it. The new **Usage breakdown** chart shows your [units](/docs/administration/billable-units) (traces, observations, and scores) as stacked bars across all projects of an organization.

- **Find the project behind a usage spike** with **By project**, then open that project to investigate.
- **See what your usage is made of** with **By type**, for example when an evaluator adds more scores than you expected.
- **Analyze the numbers offline** with **Download as CSV**, for example to split costs across teams. The file has one row per time bucket, project, and type.

Choose a preset from **Past 1 day** to **Past 1 year**, or a custom range of up to one year. Langfuse picks the bar size for you (hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly), and all buckets are in UTC.

## Where to find it

The chart counts units the same way on every deployment. Where it lives and who can open it depends on how you run Langfuse:

|                | Langfuse Cloud                                | Self-hosted                                            |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Page           | **Organization Settings > Billing**           | **Organization Settings > Usage** (new)                |
| Who can see it | Organization owners                           | Organization owners and admins                         |
| Shown with     | Your plan, invoices, and billing period usage | Usage only, as Langfuse OSS has no usage-based billing |

### Langfuse Cloud

The chart sits on the **Billing** page, below your usage in the current billing period, and counts units the same way they are billed. Langfuse Cloud has no separate **Usage** page.

### Self-hosted

The **Usage** page is part of Langfuse OSS and does not need a license key. Use it to understand your data volume, for example to size your deployment or to [estimate what Langfuse Cloud would cost](/pricing).

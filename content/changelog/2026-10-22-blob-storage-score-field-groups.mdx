Score files in scheduled blob storage exports can now include each score's metadata and where it came from. Two new field groups add these columns on top of the core score columns:

- **Join scores to their origin in your warehouse.** `score_provenance` adds `author_user_id`, `config_id`, `queue_id`, `evaluator_id`, `evaluation_rule_id`, and `execution_trace_id`, so you can break down annotation scores by reviewer and queue, or LLM-as-a-judge scores by evaluator.
- **Debug a judge's verdict.** Follow `execution_trace_id` to the trace of the evaluator run that produced the score.
- **Analyze custom score attributes.** `score_metadata` adds the `metadata` column with the key-value pairs you attached when creating the score.

Enable the groups under **Project Settings → Integrations → Blob Storage**, or add `score_metadata` and `score_provenance` to `exportFieldGroups` on [`PUT /api/public/integrations/blob-storage`](https://api.reference.langfuse.com/#tag/blobstorageintegrations). Both groups are off by default, including on new integrations, so existing score files keep their current columns. Group columns are appended after the core columns and apply to future exports only.

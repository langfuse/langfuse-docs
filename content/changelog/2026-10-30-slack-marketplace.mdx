The Langfuse Slack app is now an official app in the [Slack Marketplace](https://slack.com/marketplace/A097JJBNEB0-langfuse), reviewed by Slack. You can install it in workspaces that [only allow apps from the Slack Marketplace](https://slack.com/help/articles/1500009181142-Manage-app-settings-and-permissions) and send Langfuse notifications to the channels where your team already works:

- Post [prompt change notifications](/docs/prompt-management/features/webhooks-slack-integrations) to the channel where you coordinate releases.
- Route [alerts](/docs/observability/features/alerts) on latency, cost, or scores to your on-call channel.

If your workspace [requires app approval](https://slack.com/help/articles/222386767-Manage-app-approval-for-your-workspace), a Workspace Owner can pre-approve Langfuse in the Slack Marketplace. Then connect Slack under **Project Settings → Integrations → Slack**.

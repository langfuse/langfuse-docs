PagerDuty can receive alert, project notification, or prompt change events from the automation configuration page in Langfuse.

Paste the routing key from a PagerDuty service and Langfuse pages whoever is on call when your LLM application misbehaves:

- Trigger a `critical` PagerDuty alert when an [alert](/docs/observability/features/alerts) on p95 latency, cost, or evaluator scores crosses its threshold. The PagerDuty alert resolves on its own when the Langfuse alert recovers.
- Open an incident when a scheduled blob storage export fails or Langfuse deactivates an evaluator, by adding PagerDuty under **Project Settings → Notifications**.
- View prompt releases in your service's change timeline.

Both US and EU PagerDuty accounts are supported.

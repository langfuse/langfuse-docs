PagerDuty is now an automation action in Langfuse. Paste the routing key from a PagerDuty service or Event Orchestration into an automation, and Langfuse pages whoever is on call when your LLM application misbehaves:

- Trigger a `critical` PagerDuty alert when an [alert](/docs/observability/features/alerts) on p95 latency, cost, or evaluator scores crosses its threshold. The PagerDuty alert resolves on its own when the Langfuse alert recovers.
- Open an incident when a scheduled blob storage export fails or Langfuse deactivates an evaluator, by adding PagerDuty under **Project Settings → Notifications**.
- See prompt releases in the service's change timeline: prompt version changes are sent as change events, which never page anyone.

Each Langfuse alert maps to a single PagerDuty alert. `WARNING` triggers at `warning` severity, and escalating to `ALERT` re-triggers the same alert at `critical`. Events include PagerDuty's LLMOps custom details, such as the metric, its value, the breached threshold, and the model, plus links back to the alert and its data in Langfuse.

Both US and EU PagerDuty accounts are supported. Use **Send test event** in the automation form to check your routing key before you rely on it.

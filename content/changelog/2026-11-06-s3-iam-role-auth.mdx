Langfuse can now access your Amazon S3 buckets by assuming an IAM role in your AWS account, so you don't need to store long-lived access keys in Langfuse. You can use it for:

- [Scheduled blob storage exports](/docs/api-and-data-platform/features/export-to-blob-storage) to Amazon S3, configured in the UI or through `PUT /api/public/integrations/blob-storage` with `authType: ROLE_ARN`.
- [External Media Storage](/docs/observability/features/multi-modality#external-s3-media), which renders `s3://` media from your own bucket in traces.

Access keys remain supported and are the default. IAM role authentication is available on Langfuse Cloud Enterprise and on self-hosted Enterprise. Self-hosted deployments also need [`LANGFUSE_CROSS_ACCOUNT_ROLE_ARN`](/self-hosting/configuration#cross-account-role) set.

Amazon Bedrock is now available as a provider in the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview), next to OpenAI and Anthropic. See the [setup docs](/docs/gateway/get-started/admin-setup#add-provider-connections) to add a Bedrock credential and [Models and providers](/docs/gateway/features/models) for its API formats, then [send your first request](/docs/gateway/get-started/first-request).

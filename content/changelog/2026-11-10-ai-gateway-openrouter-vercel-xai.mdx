You can now add OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway, and xAI credentials to the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview), next to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Amazon Bedrock. OpenRouter and Vercel AI Gateway serve the OpenAI and Anthropic API formats, xAI the OpenAI formats, and the cost each provider reports is recorded on the generation. See [supported providers](/docs/gateway/features/models#supported-providers) for model IDs and setup.

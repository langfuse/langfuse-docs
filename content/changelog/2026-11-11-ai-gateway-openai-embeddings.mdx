You can now send [OpenAI Embeddings](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/embeddings/methods/create) requests through the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview) at `POST /openai/v1/embeddings`, using OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Vercel AI Gateway connections. See [supported providers](/docs/gateway/features/models#supported-providers) in [Models and providers](/docs/gateway/features/models) and the [REST API endpoints](/docs/gateway/features/rest-api#routes), then [send your first request](/docs/gateway/get-started/first-request).

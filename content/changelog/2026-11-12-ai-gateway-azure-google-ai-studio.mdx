You can now connect [Microsoft Azure (Foundry)](/docs/gateway/features/models#azure-models) to use its models through the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview), and use [Google AI Studio](/docs/gateway/features/models#supported-providers) to access Gemini models. See [Models and providers](/docs/gateway/features/models) for all supported providers, and [send your first request](/docs/gateway/get-started/first-request) to get started.

You now get more control over your [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview) keys: decide per [API key](/docs/gateway/features/api-keys#model-restrictions) which models it can access. Set [model patterns](/docs/gateway/features/models#restrict-models-per-key) such as `openai-prod:*` when you create a key, or edit them later.

You can now connect [Google Vertex AI](/docs/gateway/features/models#vertex-models) to the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview) and use Gemini and the open models Google hosts, such as Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, and gpt-oss. The gateway supports the models on Vertex AI's OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions endpoint, so Claude on Vertex AI isn't available yet.

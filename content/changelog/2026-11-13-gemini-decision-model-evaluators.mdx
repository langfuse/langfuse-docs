You can now run [decision-model evaluators](/docs/evaluation/evaluation-methods/decision-models) on `gemini-decision-1`, the model behind Google's [Gemini Decisions API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/). Select a Google AI Studio connection and `gemini-decision-1` in the evaluator, and each Choice, Score, or Yes / no question writes a score with its probabilities and confidence. For example, you can:

- flag production answers that contradict your refund policy
- route conversations to the team that should review them
- grade answer helpfulness on a rubric from `0` to `3`

Google bills only input tokens for decisions. Vertex AI connections aren't supported yet.

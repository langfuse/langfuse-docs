You can now stop traces and sessions from being shared via public links. While public sharing is off, nobody in the organization can make a trace or session public, and existing share links stop working. Turning it back on restores the links that were shared before.

**How to turn it off**

- Go to **Organization settings** → **General** and turn off **Public sharing**.
- On self-hosted instances, set `LANGFUSE_DISABLE_PUBLIC_SHARING=true` on the web container to turn off public sharing for every organization.

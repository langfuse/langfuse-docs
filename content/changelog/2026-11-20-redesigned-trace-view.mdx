The trace view looks different than it did a few months ago. We redesigned it in many small steps, so you may not have noticed each change. The video above shows the trace view before and after.

The new trace view helps you:

- See the shape of a large agent trace at a glance in the [Timeline](/changelog/2026-08-28-responsive-timeline) and [graph view](/docs/observability/features/agent-graphs), then zoom into the step that matters.
- Focus on the input, output, and metadata of the observation you are debugging, with less clutter around them.
- Work in the same layout in the full trace page, the side peek, and on smaller screens.

Read how we approached the redesign in [Comfortable with being uncomfortable](/blog/2026-11-19-comfortable-with-being-uncomfortable).

[Topics](/docs/observability/features/topics) groups your production traces into named topics and keeps them current as new traces arrive.

- **Find what you didn't know to look for.** Evals answer the questions you already know to ask. On Halo, the assistant inside Langfuse, Topics showed people writing homework and Python scripts, and a larger group trying to pen-test it.
- **See where your agent breaks.** The Issues facet names the problems that keep coming up. Filter the tracing table by a topic, read a few of its traces, then fix the prompt or add an evaluator.
- **Give your agents a map of your traces.** Agents read topics via API, MCP, and CLI, and load full traces only where they need them, which costs far less than reading raw traces.

## Four facets per trace [#facets]

Topics summarizes each trace from four angles, called facets, and clusters each one separately:

- **Intent:** what the agent was asked to do
- **Sentiment:** how the user felt
- **Outcome:** how the trace ended
- **Issues:** what went wrong

A problem spread across many intents still shows up as one topic under Issues.

## Live maps and snapshots [#maps-and-snapshots]

A [live map](/docs/observability/features/topics#maps-and-snapshots) follows the traces that match a filter and a sample rate, for example one map per agent. A snapshot clusters a time window once, for example to put your dataset traces next to production and see which use cases your datasets miss.

## Get started [#get-started]

Open **Topics** in your project and click **Set up topics**. Topics runs on models from your own [LLM connections](/docs/administration/llm-connection), and the first topics appear after about five minutes.

Topics is available on Langfuse Cloud in all regions and on self-hosted Langfuse v4. Each facet summary counts as one [billable unit](/docs/administration/billable-units), and your provider bills the model calls on your own key. Custom facets will follow soon.

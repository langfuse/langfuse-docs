You can now manage [decision-model evaluators](/docs/evaluation/evaluation-methods/decision-models#create-via-api) through the stable `/api/public/v2/evaluators` endpoints, next to LLM-as-a-Judge and code evaluators. Create them with `type: "decision_model"`, keep them in version control, and attach them to evaluation rules from your deployment pipeline.

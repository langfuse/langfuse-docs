You can now add [guardrails](/docs/gateway/features/guardrails) to the [Langfuse AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/overview) to block, redact, or log sensitive content in both requests and model responses under **AI Gateway → Guardrails**.

- **Built-in and external detection:** Match PII, secrets, prompt injection, and custom patterns out of the box, or connect an existing [Amazon Bedrock guardrail](/docs/gateway/features/guardrails#amazon-bedrock).
- **Block, redact, or log inputs and outputs:** Reject matching traffic, replace sensitive text with a label such as `[REDACTED]` before forwarding or returning (and before `FULL` trace capture), or log matches for monitoring.
- **Fine-grained targeting:** Apply guardrails to your entire organization or to specific users and API keys, scoped to the latest turn, full input, model output, or both.

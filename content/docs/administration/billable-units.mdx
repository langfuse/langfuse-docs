# Billable Units

Langfuse [pricing](/pricing) is based on the number of ingested units per billing period. Units are either [traces](/docs/observability/data-model#observations-and-traces), [observations](/docs/observability/data-model#observations-and-traces) or [scores](/docs/evaluation/scores/data-model#scores).

`Units` = `Count of Traces` + `Count of Observations` + `Count of Scores`

## Monitor organization usage [#monitor-organization-usage]

The **Usage breakdown** chart shows unit counts across your organization's projects over time. Use it to investigate usage spikes and understand which projects contribute to your total.

### Where to find it

Open your organization settings, then select the page for your deployment:

| Deployment     | Page        | Required organization role |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------------------------- |
| Langfuse Cloud | **Billing** | Owner                      |
| Self-hosted    | **Usage**   | Owner or Admin             |

On Cloud, the chart appears alongside your plan, invoices, and billing period usage. Self-hosted deployments show usage without Cloud billing information.

<Frame>
  ![Organization usage breakdown showing daily unit counts stacked by project, with By project and By type controls, a date range selector, and CSV download](/images/changelog/2026-10-06-organization-usage-breakdown.png)
</Frame>

### Explore and export usage

1. **Select a time range.** Presets span **Past 1 day** through **Past 1 year**. Custom ranges can cover up to one year. Bar intervals adjust automatically between hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly buckets, all in UTC.
2. **Choose a grouping.** Select **By project** to identify projects driving a spike, then open a project to investigate. Select **By type** to compare traces, observations, and scores.
3. **Export the data.** Use **Download as CSV** for offline analysis or allocating usage across teams. Each row represents a time bucket, project, and unit type.

For email notifications about your Langfuse Cloud bill, configure [spend alerts](/docs/administration/spend-alerts).

## Self-hosted (OSS/Enterprise)

Self-hosted Langfuse (OSS) is free under the MIT license, so there is no usage-based billing. For self-hosted Langfuse Enterprise, billable units are one component of the pricing.

The unit definition above is still useful when you want to quantify your data volume, for example to estimate the cost of moving to [Langfuse Cloud](/pricing) or to size a [self-hosted](/self-hosting) deployment.

For organization totals across projects, use the [Usage breakdown chart](#monitor-organization-usage). To inspect a single project's unit counts, you can also use the built-in **Langfuse Usage Management** dashboard, one of Langfuse's curated dashboards (see [Custom Dashboards](/docs/metrics/features/custom-dashboards)).

<Steps>

### Open the dashboard

In your Langfuse project, go to **Dashboards** and open **Langfuse Usage Management**.

<Frame>
  ![List of Langfuse dashboards including the maintained Langfuse Usage Management dashboard](/images/docs/self-hosted-usage-management-dashboards-list.png)
</Frame>

### Select the last 30 days

Set the date range to the last 30 days to capture a month of usage.

### Sum the counts

Add the count widgets together to get your monthly units:

- **Total Trace Count**
- **Total Observation Count**
- **Total Score Count** (add the numeric and categorical widgets)

<Frame>
  ![Langfuse Usage Management dashboard showing total trace, observation, and score counts](/images/docs/self-hosted-usage-management-dashboard.png)
</Frame>

</Steps>

For the example above:

`Units` = `20,070 Traces` + `119,500 Observations` + `561 Scores` = `140,131 units / month` (≈ `1,681,572 units / year`)

## FAQ

<FaqDetails>

<Details>
<Summary>How can I track my usage in Langfuse Cloud?</Summary>

Organization owners can open **Organization Settings → Billing** to [monitor organization usage](#monitor-organization-usage), compare projects and unit types, and export the data as CSV. For a single project, open **Dashboards → Langfuse Usage Management**. Configure [spend alerts](/docs/administration/spend-alerts) to receive email notifications when your Cloud bill crosses a monetary threshold.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Do units created by Langfuse features count toward billable units?</Summary>

Yes. Any trace, observation, or score stored in Langfuse counts as a billable unit, whether it is sent by your application or created by Langfuse features such as LLM-as-a-Judge, Annotation Queues, or experiments.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How can I optimize my Langfuse Cloud usage to reduce cost?</Summary>

If your application scales and you want to optimize Langfuse Cloud cost, please check out [this guide](/faq/all/cutting-costs).

</Details>

</FaqDetails>

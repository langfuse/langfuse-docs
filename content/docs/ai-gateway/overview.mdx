# AI Gateway

<AiGatewayBetaCallout />

AI is being used in many different areas of a company, with engineers using coding agents, often dozens of AI apps running in production, internally or externally. With AI usage on the rise, a company needs a way to answer questions like **"Who in our organization uses which models, for what, and what does it cost?"**

An AI Gateway enables companies to enforce AI traffic to go through one unified system, and companies get an observability platform to analyze and make decisions based on AI usage data.

## What you can do with gateway

The benefit of letting all AI traffic flow through the same entity is that you can now have a central place to observe, track and control AI usage.

You can do several things with this:

- Look into gateway data with [Analytics](/docs/ai-gateway/features/analytics)
- Pre-define a list of [available models](/docs/ai-gateway/features/models) that Gateway users are allowed to use
- Track and manage [cost](/docs/ai-gateway/features/analytics) of AI usage across your organization
- Let users self-serve [API keys](/docs/ai-gateway/features/api-keys) that work across providers, managed in one place

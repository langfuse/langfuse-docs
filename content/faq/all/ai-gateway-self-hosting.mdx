# Can I self-host the gateway?

Yes, in beta. On self-hosted Langfuse, deploy the gateway with the [Helm chart](/self-hosting/deployment/kubernetes-helm#ai-gateway) or the [Docker Compose setup](/self-hosting/deployment/docker-compose#ai-gateway). See [Self-hosting the AI Gateway](/self-hosting/configuration/ai-gateway).

# Can I get notified when a score drops?

Yes, with [alerts](/docs/observability/features/alerts). When you create an alert, choose a score data source (**Scores (numeric)**, **Scores (categorical)**, or **Scores (boolean)**), filter for your score name, and pick an aggregation and threshold, for example _average `helpfulness` below 0.7 over the last hour_. For boolean scores, the average is the share of `true` values.

When the alert fires, Langfuse notifies you through an [automation](/docs/observability/features/alerts#automations): Slack, a webhook, or PagerDuty. You can also [create alerts directly from an evaluator](/docs/observability/features/alerts#evaluator-alerts).

<Callout type="info">
  There is no webhook that fires for every individual score. Alerts evaluate aggregated score values over a time window.
</Callout>

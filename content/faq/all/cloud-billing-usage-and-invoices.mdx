# Where can I see my usage, plan, and invoices on Langfuse Cloud?

Open **Organization Settings → Billing**. This page shows:

- **Current plan and billing period.** Your plan also appears next to the organization name in the top navigation.
- **Usage** in the current billing period, in [billable units](/docs/administration/billable-units), with a usage breakdown chart. Usage is updated about once every 60 minutes.
- **Invoice history.** On paid plans, the list can start with a preview of the upcoming invoice for the current period.

From the same page you can:

- **Change plan.** Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
- **Update Billing Details** to change the payment method and billing information in the billing portal.
- **Cancel the subscription.** Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You can reactivate the subscription until then.

<Callout type="info">
  Only organization `Owner`s can view and manage billing. Other members see "Access Denied" on the billing page. See [Role-based access control](/docs/administration/rbac).
</Callout>

For plan prices and included units, see [Pricing](/pricing). To reduce usage, see [Cutting costs](/faq/all/cutting-costs).

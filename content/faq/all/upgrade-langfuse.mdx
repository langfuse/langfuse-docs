# How do I upgrade my Langfuse account?

You can upgrade via your Langfuse Organization Settings. Please navigate to your Organization --> Settings --> Billing --> Change Plans. 
From your Langfuse Project Settings, you can upgrade by navigating to your Project --> Settings --> Billing --> Change Plans. 
This is only available to Org Admins.

You can find out more about the Langfuse Pro and Teams plans on our [pricing](/pricing) and [enterprise](/enterprise) pages.

## What payment methods do you support?

We currently support credit card payments via Stripe.

## How can I change my plan / payment method?

Head to the settings page in your account and click 'manage plan'. You will be redirected to your Stripe dashboard in which you can manage your subscription and payment method.

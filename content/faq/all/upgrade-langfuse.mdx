# How do I upgrade my Langfuse account?

You can upgrade from Organization Settings. Navigate to **Organization --> Settings --> Billing**, then select **Change plan**.

From Project Settings, open **Project --> Settings --> Billing**. That link opens the same organization billing page, where you select **Change plan**.

On the Hobby plan, organization owners also see **Upgrade plan** in the sidebar. It opens the same Billing page.

<Callout type="info">
Only [organization owners](/docs/administration/rbac#reading-scopes) can view billing settings and change the plan.
</Callout>

You can find out more about the Langfuse Pro and Teams plans on our [pricing](/pricing) and [enterprise](/enterprise) pages.

## What payment methods do you support?

We currently support credit card payments via Stripe.

## How can I change my plan / payment method?

On the same Billing page, select **Change plan** to switch plans. To update your payment method, select **Update Billing Details**. That opens the Stripe customer portal, where you can manage your subscription and payment method.

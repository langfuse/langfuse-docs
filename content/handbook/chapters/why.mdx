Customers choose Langfuse because we are...

- The most used open-source AI Engineering platform [since 2024](/blog/2024-11-most-used-oss-llmops)

## Full-stack open-source [#open-source]

Langfuse is [open source](/handbook/chapters/open-source), including the UI and backend.

- [Self-host](/self-hosting) the same scalable backend that powers Langfuse Cloud. No Enterprise license needed to scale.
- All product capabilities—tracing, evaluations, prompt management, experiments, annotation, the playground, and more—are MIT licensed without any usage limits.
- We are transparent about [what's on the roadmap](/docs/roadmap) and [how we operate](/handbook).
- We iterate with the [community on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/langfuse/discussions) because best practices for building great LLM applications are rapidly evolving. Please share your feedback and ideas with us.
- Keep data on your infrastructure or let Cloud handle operations. You can move between OSS, Enterprise self-hosting, and Cloud as your needs change.

## Built for scale on ClickHouse [#built-for-scale]

We run on ClickHouse, [open source and in production for a decade](https://clickhouse.com/blog/open-source-10), with 2,000+ contributors.

- Langfuse Cloud already stores [petabytes of tracing data](/resources/engineering/clickhouse-at-agent-scale).
- With [Langfuse v4](/changelog/2026-08-17-langfuse-v4), long-range dashboards in large projects load at least 10× faster than in v3.
- Cloud and self-hosted use the same engine. Self-hosters can inspect it and query their data with standard SQL.

## Built for complex agents [#built-for-complex-use-cases]

Agents take detours. We help you follow them.

- Follow nested model calls, tools (including [MCP](/docs/observability/features/mcp-tracing)), and sub-agents through [agent graphs](/docs/observability/features/agent-graphs) and a [zoomable timeline](/changelog/2026-08-28-responsive-timeline).
- Keep conversations together with [sessions](/docs/observability/features/sessions), and add your app's context with [metadata](/docs/observability/features/metadata) and tags.
- Inspect images, audio, video, and documents in [multimodal traces](/docs/observability/features/multi-modality). Evaluate them with a compatible judge model.

See Langfuse in action for [chat agents](/chat-agents), [coding agents](/coding-agents), and [workflow automation](/workflow-automation).

## A complete platform. Start where you need us. [#comprehensive-platform]

The pieces work together. You turn a production trace into a test case, compare prompt versions, and measure the results after you ship.

- [Observability](/docs/observability/overview): understand what happened in each run.
- [Prompt management](/docs/prompt-management/overview): version, test, deploy, and roll back prompts.
- [Evaluation](/docs/evaluation/overview): run experiments on datasets and score production outputs with human review, LLM judges, or code.
- [Metrics](/docs/metrics/overview) and [data](/docs/api-and-data-platform/overview): dashboards, alerts, and exports for quality, cost, and latency.

At [Slite](/users/slite), connecting tracing and evaluations helped move experiments from quarterly to weekly.

We share how to put it all together in [Langfuse Academy](/academy).

## Security and compliance [#security-and-compliance]

<SecurityBadgesGrid />

- We take security and compliance seriously.
- **Certifications:** Langfuse Cloud has completed a [SOC 2 Type II](/security/soc2) audit and is [ISO 27001](/security/iso27001) certified. We also conduct annual third-party [penetration tests](/security/penetration-testing).
- **Privacy:** [GDPR](/security/gdpr) compliant with a [DPA](/dpa) available. For [HIPAA](/security/hipaa) workloads, eligible Pro and Enterprise customers can use a dedicated cloud region with a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) in place before processing PHI.
- **Data regions:** Choose between US, EU, Japan, or HIPAA-ready [Cloud regions](/security/data-regions), or [self-host](/self-hosting) in your own infrastructure.
- [**Hardening for Government (in development)**](/self-hosting/configuration/hardening#hardening-for-government): FIPS-compatible deployment support, hardened images, and guidance for operating the full stack in air-gapped government environments are under development for Self-Hosted Enterprise.
- **Data control:** [Data masking](/docs/observability/features/masking), [data retention](/docs/administration/data-retention), and [data deletion](/docs/administration/data-deletion), plus Cloud [encryption](/security/encryption) at rest and in transit. Configurable retention depends on plan and deployment.
- **Enterprise access:** [SSO](/docs/administration/authentication-and-sso), [fine-grained roles](/docs/administration/rbac), [SCIM provisioning](/docs/administration/scim-and-org-api), and [audit logs](/docs/administration/audit-logs), with availability depending on plan and deployment.
- More details in our [Security & Privacy Center](/security).

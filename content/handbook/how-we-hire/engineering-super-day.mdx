# Implementation review and system design

A remote 2-hour session with a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager.

<Steps>

## Preparation

Read before the session:

- The [handbook](/handbook)
- The [docs](/docs), including the data models for [observability](/docs/observability/data-model), [evaluation](/docs/evaluation/experiments/data-model), and [prompt management](/docs/prompt-and-skill-management/concepts)

## Brief

<Callout type="info">
**3-4 days prior**
</Callout>

You receive a PDF brief for one large feature. Imagine you own it at Langfuse. Think through what we should deliver to customers, how you would build it, and the biggest trade-offs. Then build a POC for the part you find most useful for making those decisions.

We'll set up a Whatsapp group where you can ask questions about the brief, the product, or the requirements.

## Session

<Callout type="info">
**2 hours**
</Callout>

You meet a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager.

Together, we review your POC and work through the product and technical requirements.

- **30min:** review of the POC you prepared.
- **90min:** system design for the broader feature, focused on trade-offs behind the biggest product and engineering decisions.

</Steps>

## [How to succeed](/handbook/how-we-hire/philosophy#what-we-look-for)

- Read the handbook and the docs before you arrive.
- Own the outcome. Make the trade-offs, and say what you would cut.
- Be direct about what works, what doesn't, and where you are unsure.

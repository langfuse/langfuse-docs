# Implementation review and system design

A 2-hour session with a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager. It is in our [office](/find-us) in Berlin. U.S. candidates join remotely.

We pay for travel and accommodation. See [Spending Money](/handbook/tools-and-processes/spending-money), book yourself, and send us the receipts.

<Steps>

## Preparation

Read before the session:

- The [handbook](/handbook)
- The [docs](/docs), including the data models for [observability](/docs/observability/data-model), [evaluation](/docs/evaluation/experiments/data-model), and [prompt management](/docs/prompt-and-skill-management/concepts)

## Brief

<Callout type="info">
**3-4 days prior**
</Callout>

You receive one brief, as a PDF. It covers the implementation you prepare and the system design in the session.

We'll set up a Whatsapp group where you can ask questions about the brief, the product, or the requirements.

Prepare the implementation before the session.

## Session

<Callout type="info">
**2 hours**
</Callout>

You meet a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager.

- **30min:** review of the implementation you prepared.
- **90min:** system design, on the same brief.

</Steps>

## How to succeed

- Read the handbook and the docs before you arrive.
- Own the outcome. Make the trade-offs, and say what you would cut.
- Be direct about what works, what doesn't, and where you are unsure.

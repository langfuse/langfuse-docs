# Engineering Super Day

The Super Day is a 2-hour morning session in our [office](/find-us) in Berlin. You meet a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager. U.S. candidates join remotely.

You bring a proof of concept of the feature. We discuss it and extend it together.

We pay for travel and accommodation. See [Spending Money](/handbook/tools-and-processes/spending-money), book yourself, and send us the receipts.

<Steps>

## Preparation

Read before the session:

- The [handbook](/handbook)
- The [docs](/docs), including the data models for [observability](/docs/observability/data-model), [evaluation](/docs/evaluation/experiments/data-model), and [prompt management](/docs/prompt-and-skill-management/concepts)

## Brief

<Callout type="info">
**3-4 days prior**
</Callout>

You'll receive a technical challenge as a PDF describing the feature.

We'll set up a Whatsapp group where you can ask questions about the challenge, the product, or the requirements.

Bring a proof of concept of that feature to the session.

## Session

<Callout type="info">
**2 hours, morning**
</Callout>

You meet a Langfuse engineer and the hiring manager. We discuss the proof of concept you brought and extend it together.

</Steps>

## How to succeed

- Read the handbook and the docs before you arrive.
- Own the outcome. Make the trade-offs, and say what you would cut.
- Be direct about what works, what doesn't, and where you are unsure.

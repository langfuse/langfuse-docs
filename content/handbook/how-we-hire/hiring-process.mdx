# Hiring process

Most of the time we are hiring for engineering roles – below you will find the process for these roles. The process might differ slightly for other roles, such as [growth hiring](/handbook/how-we-hire/growth-hiring-process).

<Steps>

## Recruiter screen

A relaxed intro call where we get to know each other and make sure the role is a good fit for you too. We'll discuss your experience and any questions you have or want to learn about Langfuse/ClickHouse.

**30min scheduled**

## Code Review

In this session, you'll work through a real code review together with one of our engineers. The goal is to see how you reason about code quality, trade-offs, and end-to-end thinking. Treat it like a collaborative conversation: we're as interested in how you spot and discuss problems as in the conclusions you reach.

**45min scheduled**

## Past Projects Deep Dive

We'll talk about your experiences. You'll chat about previous projects you contributed to and are proud of. We'd also want to understand what drives you and how Langfuse fits to your goals. How can working together have a profound impact on your career and what you want to learn by joining Langfuse.

**45min scheduled**

## Feature Building Interview

You will work with an engineer on a feature design we'd build here at Langfuse. Being familiar with the Langfuse product helps ([Overview](/docs), [Core Concepts](/docs/observability/data-model), [Free Hobby Account](/docs#where-to-start)).

**45min scheduled**

## Super Day

What the heck is a Super Day? We get that a lot. The Super Day is an onsite day in our office where we work together with you on real problems that you might work on in your future role at Langfuse. You will receive a brief ahead of time.

**1 full day** | [Engineering Super Day guide →](/handbook/how-we-hire/engineering-super-day)

## Meet ClickHouse

If you got this far, the ClickHouse leadership would love to spend a bit of time with you. This is a relaxed get-to-know before we make the offer.

**30min**

## 🥳 Yay Offer

We'll walk you through the package and terms, finalize starting dates, and ask you for 2 professional references.

</Steps>

## 'Secrets' to ace the process

- We are writing our handbook and docs for a purpose, having done your research on the product and how we work makes the interviewing process much more enjoyable for everyone.
- We are excited about people who are clear about the specific role they want to take on and have a strong take on how to shape it.

## What we look for [#what-we-look-for]

People here make a lot of decisions without anyone reviewing them, so we hire for that.

We look for people who want to go deep on one area and keep getting better at it. You should be able to think through a complex problem on your own and explain your decisions clearly in writing. In engineering, we usually look for people who have led complex work before, often as a tech lead or founding engineer. In marketing and growth, we look for strong writers with a clear take on the role they want and how they'd shape it.

See also our [hiring philosophy](/handbook/how-we-hire/philosophy).

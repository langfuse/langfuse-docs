# US Eastern remote engineering

We now hire engineers fully remotely in the United States, working US Eastern hours. This page explains what we decided, why, and how hiring works if you are based in the US.

## What we decided [#what-we-decided]

- **USA only.** We hire remote engineers based in the United States. We are not hiring in Canada or South America at this point.
- **US Eastern hours.** You work on US Eastern time. We are not hiring on US West Coast hours.
- **Fully remote.** There is no office requirement for US engineers.
- **Mixed EU/US teams.** US engineers join the same teams as our engineers in Europe. There is no separate US team.

## Why now [#why-now]

Langfuse has grown quickly, and the best engineers don't all live within reach of Berlin or San Francisco. Joining ClickHouse, a globally distributed company, gives us more support to hire beyond Europe. US Eastern hours overlap well with the European working day, which keeps one team working together without splitting into time zone silos.

## How we work across Europe and the US [#how-we-work]

- **Overlap matters.** Eastern hours give several hours of daily overlap with Europe. We use that time for discussions, pairing, and reviews.
- **Async by default.** We write things down: RFCs, decision logs, and specs. See [Working Remotely](/handbook/how-we-work/remote).
- **Meeting in person.** We still value time together. We invite everyone to our all-company offsites.

## How hiring works remotely [#how-hiring-works-remotely]

Our [hiring process](/handbook/how-we-hire/hiring-process) keeps the same bar for everyone. For US candidates, every step runs remotely:

- Recruiter screen (video call)
- Code Review (video call)
- Past Projects Deep Dive (video call)
- Feature Building Interview (video call)
- Remote working sessions instead of the onsite [Super Day](/handbook/how-we-hire/engineering-super-day): several shorter sessions where we work on real problems together
- Meet ClickHouse
- Meeting in person before an offer. Where possible, we meet finalists in person before making an offer.

## Questions? [#questions]

If you are unsure whether a role is open to US Eastern remote, check the location on the job posting or ask your recruiter.

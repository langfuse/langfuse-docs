# How we ship

We give every meaningful piece of work a clear owner, keep its status and decisions easy to find, and add only as much process as helps us deliver. These principles apply across Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Operations, and every other team at Langfuse.

- [How we use Linear](/handbook/how-we-ship/how-we-use-linear) is our company-wide agreement for planning, executing, and tracking work.
- [How engineering ships](/handbook/how-we-ship/engineering) covers the engineering-specific workflow for prioritization, specifications, implementation, rollout safety, and releases.

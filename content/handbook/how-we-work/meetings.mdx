# Meetings

We have a small number of regular meetings. Generally, standing meetings are an anti-pattern and most things are better resolved adhoc/async.

<Frame>![Meetings](/images/handbook/maker-schedule.png)</Frame>

## Weekly Meetings

### Monday: team sync

Each team runs a focused 30-minute check-in. For Engineering, the [current-quarter project view](https://linear.app/clickhouse/team/LF/projects/view/monday-current-quarter-projects-8f42f51603fa) is the agenda.

Before the meeting, project leads post their weekly update and set project health, milestone progress, and target date. Engineers keep their current-cycle issues accurate and flag blockers. We do not prepare a separate status document.

The Engineering meeting starts with a five-minute silent read. We then discuss off-track and at-risk projects, dependencies, decisions, and the week's cycle commitments. We skip unchanged on-track projects and do not do a round-robin status report. Actions get an owner and date in Linear.

See [Using Linear](/handbook/tools-and-processes/using-linear) for the project setup, update template, and full meeting agenda. Other teams can adapt the same exception-driven format to their source of truth.

### Tuesday (Biweekly): ClickHouse All-Hands

- Company-wide updates, metrics, and open Q&A with the leadership team

### Wednesday: Langfuse All-Hands

- Company-wide updates, metrics, and other topics relevant to everyone
- Goal:
  - share context across the company
  - look at metrics
  - discuss company-wide topics

### Friday: Demo All-Hands

- "What did you ship this week?"
- Full team, 60 minutes
- Everyone has 2-4 minutes
- Usually this involves screen sharing, but can also be a verbal summary.
- Not a proof of work meeting, sometimes you don't have anything to show
- Goal:
  - celebrate wins
  - share context of what is going on
  - share learnings

## Bi-monthly

**Team Retro (1h, full team, often during lunch/dinner)**

- Goal: increase our effectiveness as a team
- Everyone prepares 0-3 start/continue/stop items; Figjam board shared ahead of the session in `#team-async`
- Meeting:
  1. Read through everyone's notes, add comments when something is unclear, add 3 dot-votes to notes that are most relevant to you
  2. Resolve open comments
  3. Discuss the highest-ranked notes based on dot votes
  4. Commit to changes that we want to make as a team

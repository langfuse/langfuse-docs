# Travel & expense policy

_Last updated: 16.11.2026_

<Callout type="info">
  Only applies if you are an employee of Langfuse GmbH
</Callout>

## Remote work and offices

Langfuse is a remote team with offices in Berlin, San Francisco, Amsterdam and New York. While you can generally work from home, we believe in the importance of building culture and collaborating in-person regularly.

We hire engineers remotely across Europe (remote-first, with regular time in Berlin) and in US-East time zones. Marketing and all other roles hire in Europe and all of the United States.

European employees meet in person at the [on-sites](/handbook/how-we-work/remote#on-sites) every quarter. The Langfuse team on-sites are usually in Berlin or Amsterdam.

## On-site week

Langfuse team on-sites run Monday to Friday. These weeks are dedicated to:

- Welcoming and onboarding new joiners
- Team planning and cross-functional collaboration
- Informal relationship-building and social time

Tuesday and Thursday evenings will be loosely blocked for team dinners and team events.

You may travel during working hours and do not need to use personal time for your commute to and from the on-site. As such, we will start the week all together with lunch and end after lunch on Friday.

## Expenses

Book all travel and accommodation via Navan and follow the travel and expense policy there.

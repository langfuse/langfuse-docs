Teams building AI applications need a new workflow of continuous monitoring and evaluation to get them into production and keep improving them. Langfuse is the open-source platform for that workflow (learn more about [our mission](/handbook/chapters/mission)).

In January 2026, [we joined ClickHouse](/blog/joining-clickhouse). We still run as our own team.

We hire engineers in Europe and the US Eastern time zone. Marketing also hires in San Francisco.

If you want to build great developer tools, or get developers to love them, we'd love to hear from you.

– [Marc](https://x.com/MarcKlingen), [Clemens](https://x.com/rawert), [Max](https://x.com/maxdeichmann) and the Langfuse team

## Why join Langfuse

### We're winning the category through open source

Langfuse is the [most used open-source AI engineering platform](/blog/2024-11-most-used-oss-llmops), with the largest distribution and scale in our space. See the [public metrics](#public-metrics) below.

<CredibilitySentence bold={false} />

### AI-native companies and large enterprises

AI-native companies like [Ramp](/users/ramp) run on Langfuse, and so do very large enterprises. See more [companies building with Langfuse](/users).

<EnterpriseLogos />

### You work directly with customers, in public

Everyone talks to customers, and sometimes even customers review our RFCs before we build. Features often start as a thread in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/langfuse/discussions), and the people who build them answer users there themselves.

### You own an area and compound over time

Engineers own a feature area. Marketers own launches, events, or a content program like Langfuse Academy. You stay with your area as the team grows, and because the work is open source, your track record stays public.

### ClickHouse backs us and stays out of the way

We set our own roadmap. ClickHouse gives us a global sales team that sells Langfuse and a much larger audience for our launches, and the engineers who build the database are one message away.

### AI-pilled and remote

Everyone gets a very high AI budget. We build slides with AI, maintain our own coding harness, and almost everyone runs a small army of agents. We have three scheduled meetings a week, and everything else happens in writing (see [how we work](/handbook/how-we-work/principles)).

## What you'll work on

### Product engineering

You build features end to end that help engineers build agents, increasingly with agents:

- UI and UX for large, messy data, like the [Responsive Timeline](/changelog/2026-08-28-responsive-timeline) and the [graph view](/changelog/2026-07-13-graph-view-modes)
- [Halo](/changelog/2026-09-03-langfuse-assistant), the AI agent inside Langfuse
- Tooling for evals at scale, like [code evaluators](/blog/2026-06-22-code-evaluators-execution-model) and [experiments](/changelog/2026-10-19-experiments)

### Data platform

You own ingestion, ClickHouse query performance, and the infrastructure behind Langfuse Cloud and self-hosting, at petabyte scale. Read [how Langfuse runs ClickHouse at agent scale](/resources/engineering/clickhouse-at-agent-scale).

### Marketing and growth

Our audience is AI engineers, so most of our marketing is material they'd bookmark:

- Developer content, like the [changelog](/changelog), [Langfuse Academy](/academy), and the docs
- [Launches](/blog/2025-10-29-launch-week-4) with one owner each, which also go out through ClickHouse's much larger audience
- Product marketing, [events](/events) and community, and growth

The team ships website changes itself with coding agents.

Questions about the team or the interview process? See [questions candidates often ask](/handbook/how-we-hire/hiring-process#faq).

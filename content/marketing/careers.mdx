Teams building AI applications need to monitor and evaluate them continuously to get them into production and keep improving them. Langfuse is the open-source platform for that workflow. Read about [our mission](/handbook/chapters/mission).

In January 2026, [we joined ClickHouse](/blog/joining-clickhouse). We still run as our own team.

If you want to build great developer tools, or get developers to love them, we'd love to hear from you.

– [Marc](https://x.com/MarcKlingen), [Clemens](https://x.com/rawert), [Max](https://x.com/maxdeichmann) and the Langfuse team

## Why join Langfuse

### We're winning the category through open source

Langfuse is the [most used open-source AI engineering platform](/adoption), with the largest distribution in our space. See the [public metrics](#public-metrics).

<CredibilitySentence bold={false} />

### AI-native companies and large enterprises

AI-native companies like [Ramp](/users/ramp) run on Langfuse, and so do very large enterprises. See more [companies building with Langfuse](/users).

<EnterpriseLogos />

### You own an area, and your work is public [#you-own-an-area-and-your-work-is-public]

Engineers own a feature area and stay with it as the team grows. Marketers own launches, events, or a program like Langfuse Academy. The people who build a feature answer users in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/langfuse/discussions) themselves. Because the work is open source, your track record stays public.

You own it from the customer call to the launch. Here is how that looked for one real feature, the evals revamp:

1. **Customer.** It starts with users telling us in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/langfuse/discussions/11023) that mapping data into evaluators was painful. ([How we work with customers](/handbook/chapters/customers))
2. **RFC.** You write the RFC. The team reviews it async, then we settle the hard questions in one intense session. ([Roadmapping](/handbook/product-engineering/how-we-work/roadmapping))
3. **Build.** Then you build. A lot. The [main PR](https://github.com/langfuse/langfuse/pull/15906) touched 600 files. ([How we ship](/handbook/product-engineering/how-we-work/how-we-ship))
4. **Ship.** You launch it with the GTM team: [changelog](/changelog/2026-08-22-reusable-evaluators-and-rules), docs and marketing. ([Releases](/handbook/product-engineering/playbooks/releases))
5. **Offsite.** Then you meet everyone in person at an offsite in Tokyo or Berlin. ([Remote at Langfuse](/handbook/how-we-work/remote))

### ClickHouse backs us and stays out of the way

We set our own roadmap. ClickHouse gives us a global sales team that sells Langfuse and a much larger audience for our launches. The engineers who build the database are one message away.

Our bet is that ClickHouse will be one of the great infrastructure companies of the AI era, and that joining Langfuse now means being part of that. The database already runs real-time analytics, observability, and AI workloads. Langfuse is how that platform sees what agents do in production. For more background, [see below](#further-reading).

### AI-pilled and remote

Everyone gets a very high AI budget. We build slides with AI, maintain our own coding harness, and almost everyone runs a small army of agents. We have three scheduled meetings a week. Everything else happens in writing (see our [principles](/handbook/how-we-work/principles)).

### Marketing and growth

Our audience is AI engineers, so most of our marketing is material they'd bookmark:

- Developer content, like the [changelog](/changelog), [Langfuse Academy](/academy), and the docs
- [Launches](/blog/2025-10-29-launch-week-4) with one owner each, which also go out through ClickHouse's much larger audience
- Product marketing, [events](/events) and community, and growth

We are remote-first and hire engineers across Europe and in US-East time zones. Engineering teams are mixed EU/US. Marketing and all other roles hire in Europe and all of the United States. All roles are listed on the ClickHouse careers site. See [how we think about pay and perks](/handbook/perks-and-pay).

Hackathons are where a lot of LLM apps get their first traces. Langfuse helps you debug your agent, iterate on prompts, and show judges how your app performs, all within a weekend.

## Eligibility

You qualify if you are participating in, mentoring at, or organizing a hackathon. This is self-certified: tell us which event you're at in the form.

<Callout type="info">

Credits are meant for building at hackathons. If your project turns into a company, check out the [Startups Program](/startups).

## How it works [#apply-now]

1. **Sign up** to [Langfuse Cloud](/cloud) and start tracing. The [get started guide](/docs/observability/get-started) takes a few minutes.
2. **Apply** via the short form → [Request credits](https://forms.gle/HACKATHON_FORM_PLACEHOLDER)
3. **Receive** a coupon code by email.
4. **Activate** the coupon when you upgrade to Core or Pro via **Settings → Billing** in your Langfuse account.

## Frequently asked

<FaqDetails>

<Details>
<Summary>What happens after 3 months?</Summary>

You roll onto standard pricing. You can downgrade to the Free Plan in **Settings → Billing** at any time.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Does every team member need to apply?</Summary>

No. Credits apply to a Langfuse organization, and you can invite your whole team to it. One person per team applies.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Does the discount stack with other coupons or credits?</Summary>

No. You'll need to choose one offer.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>We're a startup / research lab / non-profit — different program?</Summary>

We have dedicated programs for other communities:

- **Startups** → [Startups Program](/startups)
- **Research & Education** → [Research Program](/research)
- **Non-profits** → [Non-Profit Program](/non-profit)

</Details>

</FaqDetails>

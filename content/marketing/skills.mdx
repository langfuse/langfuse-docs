## Point your agent at a skill [#point-your-agent-at-a-skill]

Ask it to do what the skill describes, like adding tracing to an app, calibrating a judge against human labels, or finding hidden failure modes. Select a playbook to preview the `SKILL.md` your agent reads.

<SkillBrowser />

Once the skill is installed, prompt your agent directly:

- _Show me the last 10 traces with a score below 0.5_
- _Add Langfuse tracing to the OpenAI calls in `src/agent.ts`_
- _Migrate the system prompt in `src/agent.ts` to Langfuse prompt management_
- _Create a dataset called "edge-cases" and add these three failing traces to it_
- _Calibrate my LLM-as-a-judge against the human labels in this dataset_

## Install [#install]

<AutoInstall />

The skill follows the open [Agent Skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills) standard. It is a self-contained folder with a `SKILL.md` entrypoint plus reference playbooks for specific workflows. Only the frontmatter sits in the agent's context; the full instructions load on demand.

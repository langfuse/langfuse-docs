# Langfuse for Startups

Building with LLMs is chaotic enough. Observability pricing shouldn’t add to the stress.
If your team is just getting started, **get US$2,000 in Langfuse Cloud credits for 12 months**.

> **TL;DR**
> Early-stage startup? Bootstrapped or < $5 m raised? [Fill in the form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeJqDmeiALrreznPs5e1hg60x_UDVZpD13sCbUINQ2RC09ylw/viewform?usp=publish-editor), get approved, and redeem the
> coupon code you receive to get the credits for a full year. [Apply here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeJqDmeiALrreznPs5e1hg60x_UDVZpD13sCbUINQ2RC09ylw/viewform?usp=publish-editor).

## Why we do this

We were a scrappy two-person team once, too. The early days are about finding
product-market fit, not watching your cloud bill spiral. This program makes
enterprise-grade LLM observability affordable until you’re ready to scale.

---

## Eligibility

Your company qualifies if **all** of the following are true:

| Requirement | Details                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Funding     | Bootstrapped **or** ≤ USD 5 million in total venture funding |
| Age         | Incorporated ≤ 5 years ago                                   |
| Status      | Privately held & independent                                 |
| Usage       | New or existing Langfuse Cloud customer                      |

> **Note**
> If you exceed the funding cap after approval, the credits remain valid
> for the full 12-month term—no gotchas.

---

## How it works

1. **Apply** via the short form → [Apply now](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeJqDmeiALrreznPs5e1hg60x_UDVZpD13sCbUINQ2RC09ylw/viewform?usp=publish-editor)
2. **Automatic review** (usually < 24 h). We’ll email if we need extra docs.
3. **Activate**—upgrade to a paid plan and [redeem the coupon code](/faq/all/redeem-coupon-code) you receive by email.
4. **Build!** The credits apply to your invoices for 12 months.

# HIPAA Compliance & Langfuse Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

Langfuse offers a dedicated HIPAA-ready cloud region for eligible customers with healthcare or life-sciences workloads. This region is separate from the EU, US, and Japan Langfuse Cloud regions.

Eligible customers can enter into a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Langfuse for HIPAA-regulated use. Customers with HIPAA requirements should [complete the BAA via Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/public-launch/6aa89377853bdf7f289e7139) before processing Protected Health Information (PHI) in Langfuse.

## Availability

- **Data region:** [https://hipaa.cloud.langfuse.com](https://hipaa.cloud.langfuse.com/)
- **Location:** Oregon, AWS `us-west-2`
- **Eligible plans:** Pro plan or higher

## Signed BAA

Customers on Pro plan or higher in the HIPAA data region can [complete and sign the BAA via Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/public-launch/6aa89377853bdf7f289e7139). Do not process PHI in Langfuse until the signed BAA is in place.

## How to get started

1. Create a fresh Langfuse Cloud account in the [HIPAA data region](https://langfuse.com/security/data-regions).
2. Subscribe to [Pro plan or higher](https://langfuse.com/pricing).
3. [Complete and sign the BAA via Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/public-launch/6aa89377853bdf7f289e7139) before processing PHI.
4. Configure your application to send HIPAA-regulated data only to `https://hipaa.cloud.langfuse.com`.

The HIPAA region is completely separate from the [other data regions](https://langfuse.com/security/data-regions). Data migration is possible via the [data migration cookbook](https://langfuse.com/guides/cookbook/example_data_migration).

## Self-hosted deployments

The HIPAA data region and the BAA cover Langfuse Cloud, where Langfuse operates the infrastructure. In a [self-hosted](/self-hosting) deployment Langfuse does not receive or process your data, so no BAA with Langfuse is involved and the HIPAA safeguards are yours to implement in your environment. Relevant building blocks: [encryption](/self-hosting/configuration/encryption), [data masking](/self-hosting/security/data-masking) before or at ingestion, [data retention](/docs/administration/data-retention), [audit logs](/docs/administration/audit-logs), [SSO](/self-hosting/security/authentication-and-sso), and [network isolation](/self-hosting/security/networking).

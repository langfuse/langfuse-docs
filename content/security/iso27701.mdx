# ISO/IEC 27701:2025 certification

ClickHouse, Inc. holds **ISO/IEC 27701:2025 certification** for its privacy information management system (PIMS). The scope of certificate 33323-B includes **Langfuse Cloud**.

For Langfuse Cloud, the certification covers ClickHouse’s privacy management as a processor of customer data and as a controller of sales data. For self-hosted Langfuse, the scope is limited to ClickHouse’s role as a controller of sales data for customer relationship management.

Request access to the certificate through the [ClickHouse Trust Center](https://trust.clickhouse.com/).

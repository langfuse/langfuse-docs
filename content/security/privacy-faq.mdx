# Privacy FAQ

This page addresses frequently asked questions and common privacy topics for Langfuse.

If you don't find a solution to your issue here, try using [Ask AI](/docs/ask-ai) for instant answers or reach out to privacy@clickhouse.com

<FaqDetails>

<Details>
<Summary>Do you use customer data to train AI models?</Summary>

No. Langfuse does not train or fine-tune ML/LLM models on customer data. See [Section 3.1 of the Langfuse Cloud Addendum](https://clickhouse.com/legal/langfuse-cloud-addendum) for the terms on AI Data.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>Can we enter into a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)?</Summary>

Yes. You can enter into a [DPA](https://clickhouse.com/legal/agreements/data-processing-addendum) with Langfuse.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How long is data retained?</Summary>

[Data retention](/docs/data-retention) can be defined via project‑level policies: ≥ 3 days up to unlimited. Data is purged nightly.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How can we delete data?</Summary>

Customers can delete single traces, batches, projects, organisations, or user accounts via the UI or the public API. See [data deletion documentation](/docs/data-deletion) for more details.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How do you delete data after the End of the Contract?</Summary>

Under [Section 4.1 of the Langfuse Cloud Addendum](https://clickhouse.com/legal/langfuse-cloud-addendum), customers must retrieve or delete their content before the applicable Order Form term ends. Backups, caches, or references to deleted content may take up to 30 days to be permanently deleted. Customer Personal Data is handled under [Section 3.6.2 of the DPA](https://clickhouse.com/legal/agreements/data-processing-addendum), including deletion promptly after the later of termination or expiration and any agreed post-termination retrieval period. Check your applicable agreement for customer-specific terms.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How does your data classification policy look like?</Summary>

Our Data Classification Policy organizes all information to ensure it receives the appropriate level of security based on its sensitivity. Data is categorized into three levels: Confidential for sensitive customer and employee data, Internal Use for default business information, and Public for information with no access restrictions. Each classification level has specific handling rules, such as limiting access to Confidential data on a strict need-to-know basis, to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How does your data protection policy look like?</Summary>

Our Data Protection Policy ensures all customer data at rest is stored on encrypted volumes, with direct employee access to production systems disabled by default. We enforce strict logical separation of customer data at both the database and API layers to prevent unauthorized access, while continuously monitoring our cloud infrastructure. Any necessary access to production data is granted on a temporary, need-to-know basis with explicit leadership approval, and all personnel are bound by non-disclosure agreements.

</Details>

<Details>
<Summary>How does your data retention policy look like?</Summary>

Customers can configure [project-level data retention](/docs/administration/data-retention) and use the [data deletion controls](/docs/administration/data-deletion). Retention settings, deletion from the active service, and expiry of backups are separate parts of the data lifecycle. For termination and suspension, consult the [Langfuse Cloud Addendum](https://clickhouse.com/legal/langfuse-cloud-addendum), the [DPA](https://clickhouse.com/legal/agreements/data-processing-addendum), and your applicable agreement; account suspension does not create a general contractual grace period for data retrieval.

</Details>

</FaqDetails>

## Langfuse on AWS [#integrations]

- [Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](/integrations/frameworks/amazon-agentcore): trace agents on AgentCore Runtime, including tool calls and OpenTelemetry spans.
- [Strands Box](/integrations/frameworks/strands-box): run a Strands agent in Box and forward the trace through your own collector.
- [Strands agents](/integrations/frameworks/strands-agents): instrument the Strands SDK with OpenTelemetry.
- [Amazon Bedrock](/integrations/model-providers/amazon-bedrock): traces, tokens, and cost for Bedrock model calls.
- [Amazon Bedrock Agents](/integrations/model-providers/amazon-bedrock-agents): trace a Bedrock Agents invocation together with its model calls.
- [Bedrock in the AI Gateway](/docs/gateway/features/models#supported-providers): route Bedrock calls through the Langfuse AI Gateway.
- [Self-host on AWS](/self-hosting/deployment/aws): deploy Langfuse in your own AWS account with Terraform.
